On Minimal Web Design

2026-02-01

Most modern websites are overloaded with frameworks, trackers, and dozens of third-party scripts. They load slowly and break often. A minimal website avoids these problems by design.

A plain HTML page with a small stylesheet loads in milliseconds. It works on any device, any browser, and any connection speed. There is nothing to compile and nothing to update.

This does not mean the site has to look bad. Careful use of spacing, readable fonts, and a limited color palette can produce a page that is pleasant to read without any decoration.

The constraint is useful. When you cannot rely on visual effects, you focus on the content itself. That is usually what matters.
