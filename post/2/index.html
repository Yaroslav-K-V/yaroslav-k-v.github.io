Learning in Public

2026-01-15

It is tempting to wait until you are an expert before writing about a subject. You worry that you will say something incorrect or obvious. But writing about what you learn helps you understand it better.

A short note forces you to organize your thoughts. If you cannot explain something in a few sentences, you probably do not understand it yet. Writing reveals the gaps.

It also creates a record. Months later, you can look back at what you wrote and see how your understanding has changed. That is valuable even if no one else reads it.
